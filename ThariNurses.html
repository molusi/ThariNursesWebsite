Alternative Careers

Where could your
career take you?

Your nursing degree opens more doors than you think. Click any role to explore requirements and how to get started.

📋

Case Management

Coordinate patient care and resources across healthcare settings, ensuring continuity and quality outcomes.

Requirements
  • Registered Nurse (RN) qualification
  • 2–3 years clinical experience
  • Strong communication skills
How to get started
  • Obtain Case Management Certification (CCM)
  • Network with hospital discharge teams
  • Apply to managed care organisations
🏥

Pre-Authorisation Nurse

Review and approve medical procedures for medical aid schemes — fully office-based.

Requirements
  • RN with clinical background
  • Knowledge of ICD-10 codes
  • Medical aid scheme experience (advantageous)
How to get started
  • Apply directly to medical aid schemes (Discovery, Medimed, etc.)
  • Highlight clinical assessment skills on your CV
  • Consider a short coding course
💻

Telehealth Nurse

Provide clinical support, triage, and patient education remotely via phone or video consultation.

Requirements
  • Active nursing licence
  • Reliable internet connection
  • Comfortable with digital tools
How to get started
  • Apply to telehealth platforms (Healtheon, HealthForce)
  • Build a home office setup
  • Book a consultation with Thari Nurses for guidance
🤱

Doula

Provide emotional, physical, and informational support to mothers before, during, and after childbirth.

Requirements
  • No nursing licence required (but advantageous)
  • Doula certification program
  • Passion for maternal support
How to get started
  • Complete an accredited Doula training course
  • Attend births with an experienced doula
  • Register with DONA or SAPC
🍼

Lactation Consultant

Support new mothers with breastfeeding challenges in hospitals, clinics, or private practice.

Requirements
  • Health background preferred
  • IBCLC certification (international)
  • Clinical lactation hours
How to get started
  • Accumulate required clinical hours
  • Study for the IBCLC exam
  • Partner with hospitals or open private practice
🌐

Virtual Nurse

Work entirely from home providing patient education, chronic disease management, and follow-up care.

Requirements
  • Active SANC registration
  • Strong clinical background
  • Good internet and computer skills
How to get started
  • Browse remote job boards (LinkedIn, PNet)
  • Set up a professional home office
  • Update your LinkedIn with remote-ready skills
✍️

Medical Writer

Create clinical content, patient education materials, articles, and health marketing copy.

Requirements
  • Clinical background (your greatest asset)
  • Strong written communication
  • Portfolio of writing samples
How to get started
  • Start a health blog to build your portfolio
  • Pitch to health publications
  • Freelance on platforms like Upwork
💪

Health Coach

Help clients achieve wellness goals through lifestyle guidance, nutrition, and chronic disease prevention.

Requirements
  • Nursing background is a huge advantage
  • Health coaching certification (IIN or similar)
  • Entrepreneurial mindset
How to get started
  • Enrol in a recognised health coaching course
  • Build a social media presence
  • Offer free sessions to early clients
🎓

Nurse Educator / Trainer

Train the next generation of healthcare professionals in clinical and corporate settings.

Requirements
  • RN with 3+ years clinical experience
  • Teaching qualification or experience
  • Subject matter expertise
How to get started
  • Apply to nursing colleges and healthcare companies
  • Obtain a teaching or facilitation certification
  • Develop training materials
